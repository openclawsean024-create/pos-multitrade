# Business model hypothesis

Status: hypothesis — not validated

## Buyer

Primary buyer: owner/operator of a Taiwanese micro business or small franchise.

## Value hypothesis

Reduce setup and daily friction for operators who need one workspace across food service, retail, and service profiles while preserving local data and offline operation.

## Pricing hypothesis

- Free local-first MVP for one operator and one workspace.
- Paid tier hypothesis: NT$1,499/month for multi-location / franchise workflows, subject to validation.
- Possible paid onboarding or migration service if data portability becomes a switching barrier.

## Unit-economics assumptions to test

- acquisition channel and cost per qualified store;
- onboarding time and support cost;
- activation: first sale completed and snapshot exported;
- retention: weekly active store after 4 weeks;
- conversion from free workspace to paid multi-location workflow.

No price is considered validated until a qualified operator accepts a pilot, LOI, preorder, or paid trial.
