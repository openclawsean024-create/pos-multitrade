# Demand validation

Status: not yet passed

## Qualified signals

Community comments and forum threads can establish pain and interest after deduplication and intent coding. They are not automatically purchase commitments.

The public evidence log in `customer-discovery.md` contains 10 manually checked threads across Reddit and Shopify Community. The strongest recurring signals are:

- multi-location inventory being deducted from the wrong location or going negative;
- menu, printer, ordering and payment integrations failing during live operations;
- internet outages and support delays creating material operational risk;
- staff needing simpler workflows for transfers, reporting and peak-hour checkout.

These are qualified problem signals only. They do not establish willingness to pay for POS Multi-Trade.

## Optional commercial signal

The current controller policy does not require one of the following before discovery-driven implementation:

- paid pilot;
- signed or explicit LOI;
- design-partner agreement;
- preorder or paid trial;
- qualified pilot request with a scheduled onboarding date.
