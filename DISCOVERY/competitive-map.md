# Competitive map

## Direct and adjacent alternatives

| Alternative | What customers may buy it for | Validation question for POS Multi-Trade |
|---|---|---|
| iCHEF / local restaurant POS | restaurant workflow, ordering, inventory, local integrations | Is industry switching or local portability valuable enough to switch? |
| Shopify POS | unified commerce, product/order/customer/inventory back office | Do small operators need one workspace across different business profiles? |
| Square POS | quick setup, item grid, modifiers, inventory and payments | Can a local-first product win without payment/hardware integrations? |
| Lightspeed Retail | multi-location retail inventory and reporting | Is the initial wedge smaller and simpler than a retail suite? |
| Spreadsheet / notebook / legacy POS | low cost, familiarity, no migration effort | What migration pain is severe enough to justify adoption? |

Existing detailed benchmark: `PRD/COMPETITOR-BENCHMARK.md`.

## Provisional differentiation

1. One workflow shell for food service, retail, and services.
2. Local-first operation with explicit snapshot and JSON portability.
3. International-ready workspace / locale / channel UI contract.

These are hypotheses until tested against a real switching decision.
